Home › FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, call 0723 567 812, or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your slot.

How do I use a coupon code?

Add your services to the order box, type the code (for example LUXE10) and press Apply. The discount shows before you send your order on WhatsApp.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Please call or WhatsApp us at least 24 hours before your appointment.

How can I pay?

Please confirm payment options (cash, M-Pesa or card) with our team when booking.

Do you do bridal and group bookings?

Yes! Message us the date and number of people and we will prepare a package for you.

How long does a braiding session take?

It depends on the style and length. Most braids take between 3 and 6 hours. Tell us your style when booking.

Who can I talk to if I have a problem?

Call our manager Brian Otieno on 0745 678 901 or email luxestudio@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you book or contact us we collect your name, phone number, chosen service, date and any notes you give us. When you send us a WhatsApp message or email, we receive the details you include.

How we use it

  • To confirm and manage your appointments and orders.
  • To answer your questions and give customer support.
  • To improve our services.

Cookies and local storage

We store small items in your browser, such as your light/dark choice, your cookie choice and reviews you submit on this device. You can decline cookies using the banner, or clear them in your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We only share it with the staff who serve you, or when the law requires it. WhatsApp and Google Maps are third-party services with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
